## Key points

### What askFinz is

askFinz runs its own crawled web index — pages read continuously rather than results borrowed from another engine's page.

### Who owns the answer

This is the whole argument, and it is worth stating precisely rather than loudly. The claim that everyone rents a search index is not true: Google, Brave, Amazon, Exa and a handful of others all operate their own.

### When the relay is exactly right

If you need what a specific search engine ranks right now, a relay is not a compromise — it is the correct architecture, and an owned index cannot substitute for it at any price.

## Frequently asked

### What is a SERP API?

A service that queries a search engine on your behalf, parses the results page, and returns it as structured data. It does not crawl or store the web itself.

### Do all AI products rent their search index?

No, and it would be wrong to say so. Google, Brave, Amazon, Exa and others operate their own. Most AI products built on a SERP API do rent, in effect.
