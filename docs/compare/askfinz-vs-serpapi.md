## Frequently asked

### Does SerpApi have its own search index?

No. It queries existing search engines and parses the results page. That is the product, and it is honest about it.

### Why is SerpApi so much more expensive per 1,000 than askFinz?

Different work. SerpApi pays continuously to keep scraping engines that actively resist it. askFinz answers from an index it already holds, so a query does not trigger an outbound fight with anybody.

### Can askFinz tell me my Google ranking?

No. It does not query Google and holds no view of Google's ranking.

### Is $9.17 SerpApi's real price?

It is its published rate at 30,000 searches a month, verified 12 August 2026. The entry plan is $25.00 per 1,000. Which one applies depends entirely on your volume, which is why both are shown here.
