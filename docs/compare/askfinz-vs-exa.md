## Frequently asked

### Does Exa have its own index, or is it a search-engine relay?

Exa operates its own index. It is one of the few in this comparison that does, alongside Brave, Amazon and askFinz — so the usual "renting versus owning" argument does not separate these two.

### What does Exa really cost per answer?

More than the search line alone, if your answer needs the page text. As of 12 September 2026 Exa publishes $7.00 per 1,000 standard searches covering the first ten results, $1.00 per 1,000 for each result beyond the tenth, $1.00 per 1,000 pages of content extraction and $1.00 per 1,000 pages again for AI summaries — four separate lines, each billed on its own. Answer mode is $5.00 and the deep and deep-reasoning tiers are $12.00 and $15.00. askFinz includes the passage at $0.75–$3.50 per 1,000.

### Which one is better at semantic search?

Exa has made embeddings-based ranking its headline capability and is explicit about it. askFinz retrieves by meaning too, but sells the finished answer rather than the ranking behind it, so there is no like-for-like relevance figure published on either side to compare.

### Is askFinz open source or self-hostable?

No. It is a hosted product.
