### What Microsoft Copilot is

Copilot is AI assistance integrated across Microsoft 365. Its strength is depth: it surfaces relevant emails, meeting notes, documents and SharePoint content directly inside the apps people already use.

## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz read my company's emails and documents?

No. It answers from its own index of the open web — read ahead of the question and filed by document type, so the regulation, the standard or the filing comes back as that kind of record with its identifier, alongside the passage and the address it was read from. If searching your own tenancy is the requirement, Copilot is the right tool and askFinz is not a substitute for it.

### Do I need an enterprise licence for askFinz?

No enterprise licence is required to reach the full capability set; see pricing and the beta.

### Which models does askFinz use?

Several leading families in one place, chosen per task; see which models are available.

### Does askFinz replace Power Automate?

Not as a Microsoft-ecosystem automation tool. Workflow is a pipeline builder in the same login, but it does not carry Power Automate's connectors into Microsoft services.
