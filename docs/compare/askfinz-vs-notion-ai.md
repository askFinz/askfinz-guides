## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz replace Notion?

No. It is not a document editor, a wiki or a database product. Many teams use askFinz to gather and shape material and Notion to store and share the result.

### Can Notion AI search the open web?

Yes. Research Mode searches your Notion workspace, your connected apps and the web, shows the sources it used, and can save the report as a page. The difference is what is being searched: Notion fetches from the live web as you ask, while askFinz answers from an index it has already read and filed by document type, so the same question returns a judgment, a standard or a filing as that kind of record.

### Can askFinz read my Notion pages?

It is not a Notion integration. It answers from its own index and produces output that feeds into your existing docs workflow.
