On its links-only and lighter tiers, yes — $0.80 and roughly $0.08 per 1,000 as of 7 September 2026, and askFinz has nothing at those prices. On the tier that returns page text it depends on volume: Keiro runs $2.40–$7.20 per 1,000 against askFinz's $0.75–$3.50, so Keiro's best rate is below askFinz's standalone price and above its bundled one.

### Why compare Keiro's 3-credit tier and not its 1-credit one?

Because askFinz always returns the passage. Setting a text-included rate against a text-excluded one is the comparison that makes both sides look better than they are, in opposite directions.

### Does Keiro run its own index?

Yes. Both sides of this comparison hold their own index, so ownership is not what separates them here — price and product shape are.
