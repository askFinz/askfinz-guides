## Frequently asked

### Is askFinz a Bright Data alternative?

Not as a proxy network. It sells no access to route requests through, and cannot fetch a URL on your behalf. It is an alternative only if what you actually wanted was the answer.

### Does askFinz use a proxy network?

No. It reads through real browser sessions rather than a rented proxy pool.

### Can I point askFinz at a specific site and have it crawl it?

No. It answers from the index it maintains.

### How do the prices compare?

They do not, meaningfully. Bright Data prices access to the web; askFinz prices a search against an index. No comparison figure is offered here because any would be invented.

### What happens when a site blocks automated traffic?

For Bright Data, that is the problem the product exists to solve, on your behalf, per request. For askFinz, it is handled inside its own reading, and once a page is read it does not need requesting again.
