## Frequently asked

### Does Perplexity have its own search index?

Perplexity is an answer engine built over web search. askFinz reads and holds its own index, which is the substantive difference underneath the two answers.

### Is askFinz faster than Perplexity?

Neither side publishes a benchmark that would settle it, and none is invented here. Speed is Perplexity's stated focus. The one structural difference worth naming is not a benchmark: askFinz answers from material it has already read, so the reading is not happening while you wait.

### Can askFinz cite its sources the way Perplexity does?

Yes — a question returns the matched passage alongside the answer, with the address it was read from, and Research keeps every claim tied to its source. Where it goes further is what the citation points at: because the index is filed by document type, the source can be the judgment, the standard or the study itself with its identifier, rather than a page describing one.
