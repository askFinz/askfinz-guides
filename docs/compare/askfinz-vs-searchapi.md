### What SearchAPI is

SearchAPI queries Google, Bing, YouTube and other engines on your behalf and returns the results as structured JSON, at rates that vary by engine and endpoint. It holds no index between requests; every call is a fresh relay to whichever engine you pointed it at.

### What askFinz is

askFinz runs its own crawled web index, read continuously rather than fetched per query.

### Why $1.00 is a real number and not a catch

Rates this low are worth understanding rather than dismissing. A relay has no crawl to run, no index to store and no continuous reading to fund, because none of that infrastructure belongs to it.

## Frequently asked

### Is SearchAPI cheaper than askFinz?

Sometimes. Its published range is $1.00–$4.00 per 1,000 calls depending on engine and endpoint, as of 12 August 2026; askFinz publishes $0.75 as a top-up, $1.00 with the extension and $3.50 standalone. At the top of SearchAPI's range askFinz is cheaper; at the bottom it is not.

### Does SearchAPI have its own search index?

No. It relays results from existing engines and keeps nothing between calls.

### Can askFinz search YouTube or images the way SearchAPI can?

No. Those endpoints exist because SearchAPI relays engines that have them; askFinz answers from its own index of the web.

### Why does the rate vary so much?

Because different engines and endpoints cost SearchAPI different amounts to relay. That is why a range is quoted here rather than a single flattering number.
