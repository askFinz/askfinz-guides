Almost anything text-based can be indexed for RAG: PDFs, Word documents, web pages, database records, support tickets, meeting transcripts, spreadsheets.

### RAG vs. fine-tuning

A common question is whether RAG or fine-tuning is the right approach. They solve different problems.

### What to watch out for

RAG quality depends heavily on the quality of the index. Poorly structured documents, missing context, or low-quality embeddings produce poor retrieval, and a model that receives irrelevant context often produces confidently wrong answers anyway.
