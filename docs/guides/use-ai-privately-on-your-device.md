### What on-device AI actually means

When a model runs locally, the processing happens on your hardware. Your input — the document you're summarising, the question you're asking, the text you're editing — is never transmitted anywhere.

### The practical trade-off

On-device models are capable, and they're improving quickly. They're well-suited to summarisation, drafting, editing, classification, and most language tasks that knowledge workers do every day.
