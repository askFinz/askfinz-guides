# How to get your site indexed by AI search

Waiting to be discovered isn't the only option. How to get your site read, indexed and answerable by AI search — and how to keep control of it.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/guides/get-my-site-indexed-by-ai](https://askfinz.com/guides/get-my-site-indexed-by-ai)

## Key points

### What happens without you doing anything

If your site is public and reachable, it's the kind of thing a general web crawl is built to find in the ordinary course of working through links — no submission required.

### Putting your site directly in the queue

Rather than waiting to be found, you can submit your domain directly. Doing this puts your site straight onto the list of sites waiting to be looked at — no account, nothing to sign.

### Why a whole domain, not just one page

You can suggest a single URL, but a whole domain gives an indexer far more to work with — a lone page rarely explains itself fully; the definitions live in a glossary elsewhere on the site, the caveats are in an FAQ, the real detail is a few links deeper.

### If you want deeper, priority integration

Beyond ordinary crawling and domain submission, sites that want their content read directly and kept current with priority — rather than through general crawling — can become a verified domain partner.

### Keeping control over what gets read

Getting indexed doesn't mean giving up control. Your robots.txt remains the primary lever — it states which parts of your site a crawler may read, and a well-behaved crawler follows it, including any Crawl-delay you've set.
