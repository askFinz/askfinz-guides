# AI browser extension with every model and sources

The askFinz AI browser extension puts a full AI side panel on any webpage — research, summarise, and act without leaving the tab you're already on.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/guides/ai-browser-extension](https://askfinz.com/guides/ai-browser-extension)

## Key points

### The page you're reading becomes part of the conversation

When you open the side panel on an article, report, or document, the extension reads the page alongside you. Ask it to summarise, pull out the key figures, compare two claims, or explain a term — it answers in the context of what's in front of you.

### Switch models without switching apps

The extension connects to the same models available in your full askFinz workspace. You can change the model from the panel without opening a separate tool or managing different accounts.

### It stays in sync with your workspace

Anything you want to keep — a summary, a key passage, a note you drafted — can go straight back into your askFinz workspace. When you return to the full app, your work is already there.

### Less friction means more thinking

The friction of switching tools is not just time — it breaks the line of thought. Every time you leave the page to ask a question, you risk losing the detail that made the question worth asking.
