### What a multi-model platform provides

Model access without vendor lock-in. You can switch models as they improve, as your needs change, or as costs shift — without rebuilding your workflow or learning a new tool.

### How this differs from using the APIs directly

You can access most major AI models through their own APIs — OpenAI's API, Anthropic's API, Google's Vertex AI — but doing so directly requires managing multiple accounts, multiple billing relationships, multiple authentication systems, and the engineering work of integrating each one.

### Where AI routing fits in

Some platforms go a step further with automatic routing — analysing each incoming task and selecting the best model without requiring you to choose. For a deeper look at how that works, see how AI model routing works.

### Who benefits most

Multi-model access tends to matter more as AI usage matures. Early on, one model is often good enough.
