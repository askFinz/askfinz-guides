# AI storefront native to your platform

How creators and teams use an AI storefront in askFinz to sell products, manage orders, and handle customer questions — without a separate e-commerce tool.

## Key points

### Why a separate storefront is the wrong model

If you create content, offer services, or sell digital products, you've probably signed up to at least one dedicated e-commerce platform. They're powerful, but they're islands.

### What it's built to do

Set up products without the boilerplate. Describe what you're selling and let AI help draft the listing copy, suggest pricing structure, and organise variants.

### Who this is for

Creators and educators selling courses, templates, guides, or any digital product to their existing audience. Small teams running a product alongside a service business who don't want to maintain a separate e-commerce platform.

### Connected, not bolted on

Because Shop is native to askFinz, the audience you build in Stream, the content you draft in Chat, and the products you sell in Shop all exist in the same workspace. A new product launch can move from idea to listed to promoted without the pieces living in different places.
