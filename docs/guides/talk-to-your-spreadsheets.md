# Talk to your spreadsheets with AI

How AI data analysis lets you ask questions of your numbers in plain English — no formulas, no SQL, no waiting on the one person who knows the pivot table.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/guides/talk-to-your-spreadsheets](https://askfinz.com/guides/talk-to-your-spreadsheets)

## Key points

### Why most people can't use their own data

Spreadsheets are powerful for the people who know them well. For everyone else they are opaque.

### What AI data analysis actually does

It closes the gap between the person who has a question and the data that could answer it. You describe what you want to know; the system reads the data and responds.

### The questions that tend to get skipped

In most teams there is an informal ranking of questions: the ones worth the effort of asking the data person, and the smaller ones that get answered by instinct instead. That informal ranking is costing you accuracy.

### Where this fits alongside your other work

Data analysis rarely lives in isolation. The number you surface usually leads to a document, a conversation, or a decision that needs backing.
