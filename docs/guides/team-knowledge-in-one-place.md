### What gets lost when knowledge isn't shared

When institutional knowledge isn't written down and findable, teams pay for it in ways that are easy to overlook: repeated work, slow onboarding, inconsistent answers to the same question, decisions made without the context that already existed.

Knowledge, coming to askFinz, is being built to be where your team's documents, notes, and saved research live — and where anyone on the team can search them in plain language, the same way they'd ask a question.
