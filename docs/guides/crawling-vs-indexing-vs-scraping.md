# Crawling vs indexing vs scraping

Crawling, indexing and scraping get used as synonyms, but they're three different jobs. A plain-English breakdown of what each one actually does.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/guides/crawling-vs-indexing-vs-scraping](https://askfinz.com/guides/crawling-vs-indexing-vs-scraping)

## Key points

### Crawling: finding pages

A crawler's job is discovery. It starts from a known page, reads the links on it, and follows them to find more pages — then repeats that process outward, page after page, site after site.

### Scraping: pulling out specific data

Scraping is narrower and more surgical. Instead of asking "what pages exist," scraping asks "what specific values are on this one page" — a price, a phone number, a table of numbers, a list of names.

### Indexing: making it findable

Indexing is the step that turns "we have a pile of pages" into "you can ask a question and get an answer." It takes what crawling found — and, where documents are involved, what scraping or a similar extraction step pulled out — and organises it so it can be searched instantly, rather than re-read…
